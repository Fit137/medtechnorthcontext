### Worked example of a single entry

> ### 1. Distributors will switch carriers on estimate accuracy rather than on price
>
> **Category:** Demand · **Load:** 9 · **Odds wrong:** 6 · **Hidden time:** 11 months · **Pressure:** 54 · **Status:** Untested
>
> **If this is wrong:** the product is a reporting tool inside an existing relationship rather than a reason to switch, which caps the price at a small monthly fee and removes the expansion case the plan rests on.
>
> **Evidence for:** eleven discovery calls in March and April where accuracy was raised unprompted by seven people.
>
> **Evidence against:** no distributor has changed carrier in the observation period. The two paying customers bought reporting and kept their existing carrier.
>
> **What was said:** "they all complain about it, but I have not actually seen anyone leave over it."
>
> **Why it is scored this way:** stated frustration is well evidenced and switching behaviour is not evidenced at all, so the evidence supports a weaker claim than the one the plan uses. Hidden time is long because renewals look healthy either way until an expansion conversation fails.
