# ADR-005 — No pricing on the public website

**Status:** Accepted, with an open question

## Decision
Membership and partnership are both application-based. Pricing is discussed once mutual fit is confirmed.

## Why
For an audience of senior clinicians and decision makers, a price list frames the network as a product being sold rather than a room being composed. It also invites a clinician to calculate what founders pay to sit near them, which is the one thought that damages the trust the model rests on.

## Consequence
The builders page shows four tier names described by scope, with no numbers and no comparison table with checkmarks.

## Open
A full public pricing table and savings scenarios exist and are ready. Publishing them is a strategic call that reverses a founding principle of the page. Deferred, not decided.
