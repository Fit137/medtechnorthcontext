# ADR-011 — Insight brief as the first product sold

**Status:** Accepted

## Decision
The category insight brief is the first revenue product, ahead of advisory roundtables, sponsored panels and any event sponsorship.

## Why
It requires no venue, no physicians, no compliance battle, no event logistics and no track record. The raw material is a byproduct of rooms already being run. Compliance treats it as market research, which is a low-friction category companies already have budget lines for.

It is also the **proof artifact** that makes every other product sellable.

## Scoring
8.20 weighted, second only to the sponsored panel series at 8.35, and ahead of advisory roundtables at 7.95. It scores 10 on clinician trust and 10 on compliance feasibility, the two highest-risk dimensions.

## Two sellable immediately
- *What Ontario pharmacists need to operationalise the July 2026 scope expansion*
- *How Ontario dental practices are adapting to the 2026-27 CDCP benefit year*

Both target sectors that had structural change on 1 July 2026, three weeks before the first room.

## Price
$10–25K commissioned with 90-day exclusivity, or $2.5–5K syndicated to four to eight buyers.
