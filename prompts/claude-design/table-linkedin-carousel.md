# Prompt: LinkedIn carousel for The MedTech North Table

> **Run this in the same Claude Design project as the partner deck**, and after the Luma cover, so the carousel has a sibling to match rather than nothing to anchor to. See `assets/design/design-system-tokens.md` for why a prompt cannot carry a design system.
>
> Copy mirrors the live Luma page exactly. Six frames, not eight: every edit Ali has made at publish has been a cut.

---

Build a six-frame LinkedIn carousel for **The MedTech North Table**, a small monthly dinner in Mississauga for people who work in health tech. Each frame its own artboard, individually exportable as PNG.

**Match the Luma cover for this series, and behind it the MedTech North partner deck.** Same tokens, same palette, same restraint. The carousel and the cover should read as one set. If anything below conflicts with the project's design system, the design system wins.

**Format:** 1080 × 1350, all six frames.

**Type:** `var(--font-display)` for headline copy, `var(--font-ui)` for support and footers. Do not name or substitute a typeface.

**Colour:** ink `#16191d`, the gray family through `#8a9299`, light neutrals, white. Red `#c41230` on frame 3 only, and on the link line of frame 6. The system is cool. No cream, no beige, no warm neutrals.

**Copy. Typeset exactly, do not rewrite, shorten or add a word.**

| # | Copy |
|---|---|
| 1 | *The MedTech North Table*<br>*Table #3*<br>*Mississauga, Wednesday 26 August* |
| 2 | *A small dinner for people who work in health tech.* |
| 3 | *No pitch. No slides. Nobody selling anything.* |
| 4 | *There's no programme. Nothing to prepare, nothing to present.* |
| 5 | *Founders, clinicians, practice owners, researchers. Kept mixed on purpose.* |
| 6 | *Wednesday 26 August, 6:30. No cost.*<br>*Link below* |

**Rhythm.** Frame 1 is the cover and carries the drawn long table, empty and simply set, in the deck's geometric register. **Frame 3 is the peak and takes the largest type in the set**, three short lines stacked with generous leading, no illustration. Frame 4 sits just under it. Frames 2, 5 and 6 are quiet, at roughly 40% of frame 3's size. Someone swiping should feel it lift at 3 and settle after.

**Footer on frames 2 through 6:** the MedTech North mark and wordmark small on the left, "Mississauga" on the right, in `var(--font-ui)`. Frame 1 carries the series name at headline scale and takes no footer.

**Three constraints that are not style choices and cannot be relaxed:**

- **No people, faces or hands in any frame.** An image implying attendance at a past event is a claim that cannot be evidenced
- **No camera, tripod, light or recording equipment**
- **No pens, cards, notebooks, laptops or phones.** Nothing is asked of anyone at this dinner, and an object implying a task contradicts that
