## Three decisions the data forces

**1. The registration category cannot go on a slide.** The form's "Ecosystem contribution" field returned self-selected categories, not contributions. Three of fourteen answered "Other", which says nothing on a wall in front of a room. Two answered "Consultant", and `CLAUDE.md` rule 5 forbids Consultant as a badge or role label, which is exactly what a printed category is. So the category does one job only, which is to pick the group marker shape. The contribution line is written, not harvested. Fourteen of them are in Part B.

**2. "What you are working on" comes off the slide.** It was going to be a written line. It should not be, for two reasons. Six of these organisations are early enough that I have no verifiable idea what they build, and inventing a line about someone's company and putting it on a wall behind them is the one error that cannot be walked back in the room. Second, they are standing up to say it. The slide identifies the person. The three questions stay empty and get spoken, except the word.

So each slide carries one written line, the ecosystem contribution, and it is written from what each person put in their own registration. Nothing else about their work is asserted.

**3. Expect nine to twelve speakers, not fourteen.** Six of the fourteen paid nothing after a full discount code, one paid half, seven paid in full. The partner deck's own working assumption is that free attendance runs 40 to 60% no-show, and room 002 lost three confirmed physicians on the day. Build all fourteen slides. Person slides carry no visible numbering, so an absent person is skipped in silence.

# PART D. Slide anatomy, revised

```
  ┌────────────┐   AMR KAYID
  │            │   Cofounder · EESI
  │  headshot  │
  │            │   ECOSYSTEM CONTRIBUTION
  └────────────┘   Cofounder of an early-stage company.      [ company logo ]

  ──────────────────────────────── seam ─────────────────────────── [ marker ]

  WORKING ON

  WHY CANADA

  THE WORD         ONE WORD

  · · · · ·                                  MedTech North │ Lugu │ leaf
```

**What is written:** name, title, organisation, one contribution line. That is all.

**What is spoken:** what they are working on, and why Canada. Those two labels sit on the slide with empty space beside them, on purpose. Do not fill them, and do not add a placeholder rule to either, because a rule reads as a missing value rather than as a held space.

**What lands live:** the word, typed into the slide during or after their slot.

**Four things every slide carries identically:** the headshot frame, the logo box, the marker, and the footer rail with the three marks. Same size, same position, every slide, no exceptions. This is the rule that keeps a professor's slide and a pre-seed founder's slide structurally identical, which `CLAUDE.md` rule 6 requires and `ADR-023` already paid for learning.

## The three constant marks

MedTech North wordmark, Lugu mark, and a maple leaf, in that order, in a footer rail at the same position on every slide in the deck.

- All three at equal optical weight, hairline separated, small. A rail, not a banner
- MedTech North and Lugu render in ink, not in colour, so neither outranks the other
- **The maple leaf is drawn as SVG in brand red `#c41230`.** Not the national flag, not the Government of Canada wordmark, not an emoji, not a stock asset. A drawn leaf mark sidesteps flag protocol entirely and it cannot be read as government endorsement, which rule 7 forbids
- The leaf is the only red on a person slide. Nothing else on a person slide is red
- **Exception:** on slide 1 and on the closing ask slide, where a red line of copy carries the ask, the leaf renders in ink. One red emphasis per surface, always

## Headshots and logos, and why they are treated the way they are

These arrive from fourteen different people at fourteen different levels of quality: a studio portrait, a cropped wedding photo, a selfie, a LinkedIn thumbnail at 200 pixels.

**Render every headshot monochrome**, as a duotone in the ink family. A bright studio portrait next to a dim phone photo is a visible ranking of the two people, and colour is what makes it visible. Monochrome puts them on the same level, which is the identical-geometry rule applied to images rather than to boxes.

**Render every logo in single-colour ink**, matched by cap height rather than by bounding box, which is how a logo set is normalised. A full-colour corporate mark beside a plain wordmark creates the same hierarchy the headshot rule removes, and on this list it would put Dynacare and DMZ above an independent operator. Any logo that collapses in one colour is dropped and the organisation is typeset instead. That is not a downgrade, and on several of these slides it will look better.

**Placeholders must never look broken.** No missing asset, at any point tomorrow evening, may leave a grey box on the wall.

- No headshot: the person's initials, in the display face, inside the same hairline square, optically centred
- No logo: the organisation name typeset in the UI face at the logo box's cap height
- No word yet: a hairline rule about eight characters wide

**Grid, at 1920 x 1080:**

- **Headshot**, left, square, 420 x 420, no corner radius, a 1 px hairline rule in the light gray family. Rendered monochrome as a duotone in the ink family. Never a circle, never a drop shadow, never a gradient edge
- **Name**, right of the headshot, `var(--font-display)`, the largest type on the slide
- **Title and organisation**, one line beneath, UI face, gray, separated by a middle dot. **Typeset verbatim from the data attributes.** Never abbreviate, expand, retitle, reorder or recapitalise. Where the organisation is empty, print the title alone and close the gap, with no separator left hanging
- **Label** `ECOSYSTEM CONTRIBUTION`, small, letter-spaced, gray
- **Contribution**, one line, UI face, ink. One line only. If a line overflows, it is the copy that gets cut, not the type size
- **Logo box**, right of the contribution line, 64 px tall, width free up to 320 px, single-colour ink, optically centred on the contribution baseline
- **Marker**, right end of the seam, drawn SVG, identical size and position on every slide. Four shapes: gear, medical cross, rising bars, columns. **Shape only. Never colour, never size**
- **The seam**, full width, at the permanent vertical position it holds on every slide in the deck
- **Below the seam, three labelled rows**, left label column, content column to the right:
  - `WORKING ON`, **label only, held empty. Add nothing, not a rule, not a prompt, not a placeholder**
  - `WHY CANADA`, **label only, held empty. Same**
  - `THE WORD`, with `data-word` set as the second largest type on the slide
- **Timer**, five hairline dots sitting on the seam, left aligned, one per minute, filling as the slide is shown. The fifth dot fills red
- **Footer rail**, bottom, constant on every slide in the deck: MedTech North wordmark, Lugu mark, maple leaf, left to right, hairline separators, equal optical weight, small. MedTech North and Lugu in ink. The leaf drawn as SVG in `#c41230`
- **Nothing else.** No slide number, no page count, no progress bar, no date, no QR code, no social handle, no decorative field

## Asset paths and placeholders

Wire `data-headshot` and `data-logo` to paths under `assets/headshots/` and `assets/logos/`. The files will arrive later. **Every placeholder must look deliberate:**

- Empty headshot: the person's initials, display face, optically centred in the same hairline square. No grey fill, no silhouette icon, no camera glyph
- Empty logo: the organisation name typeset in the UI face at the logo box's cap height
- Empty word: a hairline rule about eight characters wide

## Two behaviours

1. **`data-word` is `contenteditable`**, with no visible affordance until focused, so a word can be typed during the evening. The word wall reads from these fields, so a word typed on a person slide appears on the wall with no second pass
2. **The timer starts when the slide becomes visible and resets on leaving it.** `T` pauses and resumes. It must be possible to present with the timer never noticed

## Hidden and empty slides

- The 7 hidden standby slides are fully built and marked `hidden`, reachable by a key rather than by arrow navigation, so they never appear by accident during the programme
- The 4 empty slides are the template with placeholders in place and every data attribute blank. They must look complete when a name is typed in and nothing else is
