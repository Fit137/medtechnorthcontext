# Convening Conversion Path

> **INTERNAL. Never shown to an attendee.** How a Builders Dinner produces a signed convening. Format decision: `ADR-025`. Product and pricing: `docs/16-product-architecture.md`.

---

## The target is the Brief, not the convening

**$4,500, credited in full against the first convening.** That is the first signature to chase, and framing it as the goal changes every decision below.

Chasing $25,000 from a standing start with two Miro rooms behind you is a nine-month sale. The Brief is a different purchase: small enough to be a marketing manager's decision, credited so it costs nothing if they proceed, and honest in a way that closes doubt. If we cannot fill their room we tell them at brief stage and they spent $4,500 instead of $25,000 (`ADR-022`).

**Stated target: one Brief signed by the third dinner.** Six qualified buyer seats an evening, eighteen conversations across three dinners. One signature out of eighteen is not ambitious.

## Who occupies a free seat

Free seats are the pipeline, so they are filled deliberately. Run every candidate through the firmographic screen in `data/corporate-targets.md` before the invitation goes out.

| Qualifies | Does not |
|---|---|
| Series B and later health companies with a Canadian commercial team | Pre-revenue anything |
| Canadian country offices of multinationals, holding their own P&L | Global brand teams with no Canadian line |
| Corporate venture arms whose parent sells into clinicians | Pure financial VCs with no operating company behind them |
| Scaled operators now running commercial somewhere larger | Advisors, consultants and fractional anyone |
| Anyone with a professional relations, clinical education, market development or KOL function and no community of their own | Companies whose buyer is a hospital procurement committee |

**The highest-signal indicator is unchanged: a company that already rents access to clinicians.** They have the budget line, they have the habit, and they are dissatisfied with what they are currently renting.

Six to eight free seats per dinner. At least four must clear the screen. The rest can be pure draw.

## Why the seating chart is the sales act

**The buyer is seated at the table whose founders are describing that buyer's own market back to them.**

A market access lead at a multinational spends forty minutes hearing five founders articulate exactly where the Canadian adoption pathway breaks, in language nobody inside their company uses, from people they cannot normally get on a call. Nothing is pitched. Nothing needs to be.

By dessert they have concluded on their own that we can assemble a room they cannot. **That conclusion is the entire sale, and it cannot be produced by saying it out loud.**

Pitching from the floor ends an invitation permanently (`ADR-020`). That applies to us most of all.

## The blocker round is the demonstration

Each table runs one structured round: every founder gets five focused minutes on their current blocker, with feedback from the table and from the investor or operator seated there.

It does four things at once.

1. It is the value the paying room came for
2. It gives the qualified buyer a live, unfiltered read on their own market
3. It produces forty ranked blockers an evening at zero marginal cost
4. Three dinners of it is an insight brief (`ADR-011`), which is the artifact that proves we can hear a market

**Log every blocker the same night, tagged by company stage and category.** The aggregate is worth more than any single evening and it is the only thing here that compounds.

## The follow-up, in four moves

Timing matters more than wording. All four inside ten days.

### Move 1, day +2. The note, not the pitch

A short private note to each qualified buyer. What was heard at their table. Three companies blocked on the same thing, and the pattern underneath it. No offer, no deck, no mention of convening, no ask.

**This note is the product demonstration.** It proves the thing they are actually assessing, which is whether we can hear a market and report it honestly. A composition report is not available here because a Builders Dinner promises no composition, so the note does that job instead.

### Move 2, day +5. The question that is already the Brief

One question, by message or on a short call:

> **If you could put fifteen of the right people in a room and ask them one question, what would the question be?**

That is the Brief, asked in a form nobody experiences as a sale. **If they answer it seriously, they have started buying.** If they answer vaguely or deflect, they are not a buyer this quarter and they move to nurture. Either way the answer is the qualification.

### Move 3, day +8. Name the thing

Only for the ones who answered. The convening exists, here is what it is, here is what the Brief costs and that it is credited in full. Send `docs/16-product-architecture.md` pricing, not the partner deck (`docs/15-partner-deck-audit.md`).

**Say the guarantee out loud, early.** If we cannot fill it we tell them at brief stage. That sentence closes more doubt than any proof of track record we currently possess, and it is the honest position anyway.

### Move 4, day +10. The next dinner

Everyone who did not convert gets a free seat at the next edition. It costs $105 and keeps a qualified buyer in the room while their timing changes. **Nobody is ever dropped for saying no once.**

## Two things that would break this

**Selling in the room.** One overt pitch at a table and the evening becomes a vendor event, the founders stop paying, and the pipeline dies with the product. The room is a demonstration, never a meeting.

**Selling composition off a Builders Dinner.** This room has no composition promise and produces no composition report. A buyer who signs a convening because they enjoyed a founder dinner has bought a different product and will say so afterwards. **State the difference in move 3, in writing, before any signature.**

## What to measure per dinner

| Metric | Target |
|---|---|
| Free seats clearing the firmographic screen | 4 of 6 |
| Notes sent by day +2 | 100% of qualified buyers |
| Brief questions answered seriously | 2 of 6 |
| Briefs signed, cumulative by dinner three | **1** |
| Blockers logged | 35+ |

Log all of it in the relationship graph. A qualified buyer who attends three dinners without signing is a stronger prospect than a stranger, and only the record will show it.
