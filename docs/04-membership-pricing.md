### Tier naming history
Originally proposed as the clinical ladder (Guest/Resident/Attending/Chief). **Rejected** because those are regulated medical titles and the core audience would notice a founder wearing "Chief" beside an actual Chief of Staff. "Host" was rejected for the top tier because it implies obligation rather than perk. See `ADR-006`.

**The mechanic:** low-ticket items (dinners) are included outright because inclusion drives attendance and attendance is the product. High-ticket items (summit, booth) are discounted rather than included, so heavy commercial users still contribute revenue in proportion to what they extract.

## The calibration story

**Four dinner tickets at the door cost $280. Chapter membership is $290 and includes all four**, plus cohorts, directory, introductions, partner benefits and a member rate on everything else. You pay roughly the price of the dinners you were already attending and the rest comes free.

**The upgrade math sells National:** Chapter plus $500, of which the summit pass alone is $395, plus two away dinners ($140) plus the exhibitor rate.

## Perks by tier

Fourteen of thirty perks cost nothing marginal to deliver. That is how the value stack reaches 4–5× the fee without eroding margin.

**Guest+:** directory profile with pitch media · nomination rights · dinner invitations when composition calls · live cohorts · annual composition report · digital member badge

**Chapter+:** guaranteed home-province seats · recorded cohort library · physical member card · welcome kit · member rate on summit and booth · seat-preference requests · **partner benefits bundle** (cloud credits, an hour of IP or regulatory counsel, a banking offer — funded by category partners, zero cost, can exceed the fee on its own) · early access · 2 introductions per quarter

**National+:** summit pass included · 2 away dinners · cohort moderator eligibility · speaking consideration · 1 guest pass · unlimited introductions

**Circle:** every dinner every city · 2 summit passes · booth included · co-host a dinner · member wall · $250 printables credit · summit programme input

## Terms the pricing page must carry

- **Composition clause.** Every seat at every tier, including Circle, is subject to the balance of the room.
- **Monthly guard.** A monthly member becomes eligible for an included dinner seat after 60 days, or pays the $35 rate. Without this someone joins for $29, takes a $70 seat and cancels.
- **Guest eligibility.** No commercial product or service being sold. Reviewed at application. A Guest who founds a company moves to a paid tier at renewal with a 90-day grace period.
- **Refunds and transfers.** Non-refundable, but a seat transfers to a colleague free with 72 hours notice.
- **No-shows.** Two unattended reserved seats in a year moves a member to the waitlist for the following quarter.
