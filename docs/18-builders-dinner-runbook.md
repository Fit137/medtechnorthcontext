## 1. The night

Weeknight, 6:30 to 9:30 pm, one restaurant, 24 to 48 people, five tables of eight.

| Time | What happens |
|---|---|
| 6:30 | Arrival. Portrait and film station lit from the first minute |
| 7:00 | Seated to plan. Two minutes of welcome, no deck |
| 7:10 | **The conversation.** One named operator, interviewed by Ali, 30 minutes, no slides |
| 7:45 | Dinner served |
| 8:15 | **The blocker round.** Five minutes per founder at each table, one investor or scaled operator seated at every table |
| 9:15 | Close. Stated finish honoured |

**No pitching from the floor, no demos, no stage time for anyone paying.** Enforced the same way as `ADR-020`: a breach ends the invitation permanently, and that applies to us first.

## 2. The two draws

**The named operator.** Someone who has actually built or sold a health company in Canada, interviewed for 30 minutes. No fee, dinner and courtesy only. The ask converts because it is a conversation with nothing to prepare.

**The named investors.** Three to five health investors, present and named in the invitation with written permission. **This is the single strongest fill mechanism available with a founder audience** and it beats any description of the evening. Sources: MaRS IAF, Lumira, Genesys Capital, Radical, CTI Life Sciences, Relay, Golden, plus the angel orbit around MaRS and JLABS.

## 3. Who is in the room

**Paying room: seed and pre-Series-A health company founders and operators.** Stage-homogeneous on purpose. Sourcing is a weekend from MaRS health portfolio, JLABS Toronto, CDL-Toronto health and matter, H2i, DMZ health cohorts, OBIO, Life Sciences Ontario, and Crunchbase filtered to Toronto health at seed and above. Four hundred names available, not forty.

**Sector filter.** Medtech, digital health, clinical AI, remote monitoring, D2C health apps. **Exclude preclinical biotech and pharma.** They sell to regulators, payers and pharma BD, not to anyone in this room, and they will not return.

**Free seats: 6 to 8**, filled against the firmographic screen in `docs/20-convening-conversion-path.md`. At least four must clear it.

### Why two tiers rather than one flat ticket

A cold founder dinner from an unrecognised brand supports about $150. Against a $105 landed seat, $150 will not carry a media operation. **The $250 tier mostly exists to make $550 read as correct.** A 60 second edited film quotes at $800 to $1,200 standalone, and the marginal cost of the fourteenth film on a night the crew is already hired is close to zero. That gap is the margin.

## 5. Go or no-go

The media team is the earliest committed cost, so both gates sit around it.

> **Gate 1: $4,500 cleared by day −14.** Covers food and media for 24. Below it, postpone and refund in full within 48 hours.
>
> **Gate 2: $8,000 cleared by day −7.** Expand the guarantee from 24 to 40.

**Book the restaurant at a guaranteed 24 with a written option to expand to 48 up to day −10.** Most GTA restaurants agree to this on a slow weeknight. It means the evening cannot lose money regardless of how sales go, which is the point of an MVP.

## 6. Contracting the restaurant

1. **Guaranteed minimum 24, option to 48 at day −10, in writing.** The most valuable line in the agreement
2. **Food and beverage minimum, not a room rental fee.** Spend counts toward it, so the risk sits with the restaurant
3. **Final count at 72 hours**
4. **A held per-head rate across a recurring booking.** Six weeknights is real negotiating power on a slow night and moves $105 toward $85
5. **Written answer on whether they require a certificate of insurance.** If yes, insurance becomes a hard blocker rather than a background task

Five round tables of eight, not a long table. The blocker round needs tables that can hold their own conversation. Confirm there is a corner that can take a lit station without blocking service.

## 8. Media operation

One photographer and one videographer, four hours. One lit corner, a backdrop that is not a step-and-repeat, one wireless mic for the conversation.

**Everyone leaves with three edited portraits, paid or not.** That is what makes a camera welcome rather than extractive.

**$550 buyers additionally get a 60 second film**, same station, same single question for everyone, no preparation. One identical question produces a consistent series and removes preparation anxiety, the same discipline as `ADR-023`.

### Consent, on paper, before the first frame

One paragraph signed at check-in. Covers the attendee's own image, names the edition and date, refusable without any effect on attendance. **A red dot on the name card marks anyone who declined and the crew is briefed to frame them out.**

Per `ADR-025` section 7: depicting is allowed, tallying is not. No headcounts, no totals, no counters, no image implying endorsement.

## 9. What to carry out of every dinner

Three things, logged the same night or they evaporate.

1. **Every blocker**, tagged by stage and category. Three dinners is an insight brief
2. **Every qualified buyer conversation**, into the relationship graph
3. **Consent status** for every attendee, so the media is usable without chasing anyone

**The dinner is not the deliverable. The pipeline is.** An evening that runs beautifully and produces no logged blockers and no buyer notes has failed at the thing it exists for.
