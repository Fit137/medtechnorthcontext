## The two who did not come — the most valuable data of the night

**A confirmed MD speaker withdrew on learning the venue was a residential unit.** For a physician, venue is a legitimacy test before it is a logistics detail. This was a known risk. It is now a measured cost.

**An MD founder, whose app targets doctors, asked how many physicians would attend and withdrew when told the count was low.** He was not coming to meet founders or students. He was coming for peer density.

**The inference:** the model assumed clinicians come to see what is being built. This suggests **MDs come for other MDs**, and everything else is secondary. If that holds, MD acquisition is a cold-start problem that only breaks with critical mass committed before the next invitation goes out.

## What worked

- Attendees gave positive feedback and were keen to return
- Cross-disciplinary friction surfaced: a dentist and a clinical pharmacist read the same patient journey differently, and both readings were correct
- The Patient Journey Jam format produced a tangible artifact for presenters
- Miro as a live collaborative canvas worked well and was identified as an under-used format in healthcare

## What did not

- Zero physician attendance
- The venue actively repelled the audience the network was designed around

## Decisions taken

- **Service providers were turned away.** Admitting them would have at least doubled headcount. This was correct: eight service providers in a room of sixteen is a vendor floor with a few clinicians in it
- Kept unsponsored and free

---

## The strategic conclusion

This is a clinician-dense room with a physician gap, not a failed room.

**Dentists and clinical pharmacists are not a consolation prize.** In buying-cycle terms they may be the stronger room: a dentist owns a practice and can buy in three weeks, a pharmacist works inside a chain or independent with real budget and expanding scope. A hospital MD sits inside the slowest procurement machinery in the country.

Both groups had a structural change on 1 July 2026, three weeks before this event. See `../verified-stats.md`.

**Decision: monetise the room that exists, and let it fund physician recruitment later.** See `../../decisions/ADR-012.md`.
