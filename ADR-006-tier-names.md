# ADR-006 — Tier names: Guest, Chapter, National, Circle

**Status:** Accepted

## History
Three naming sets were considered.

**Set A, the clinical ladder: Guest / Resident / Attending / Chief.** Initially recommended and scored highest at 9.15. It borrows the hospital career hierarchy so clinicians read the ladder instantly, and "Attending" is a genuine double meaning.

**REJECTED on compliance grounds.** Resident, Attending, Chief and Fellow are regulated or restricted titles tied to clinical rank and licensure. Beyond the regulatory issue, the core audience would notice a founder wearing "Chief" on a badge beside an actual Chief of Staff. Reputational damage with clinicians is the one cost this business cannot absorb.

**Set B: Guest / Chapter / National / Host.** "Host" rejected because it implies an obligation rather than a perk, which is the antithesis of a marketed benefit.

**Set C, accepted: Guest / Chapter / National / Circle.**

## Also rejected
"Founding Circle" as the top tier name. Three problems: it is time-bound language on a permanent tier, it collides with the founding-member rate for the first 100, and it collides with "Founders" as an ICP segment, which tilts the ladder toward one group by construction.

## Constraint for all future naming
Never use as a tier, badge, role label or member designation: **Resident, Attending, Chief, Fellow, Chair, Registrar, Consultant, Specialist, Practitioner, Associate.** "Consultant" in particular means a senior hospital physician in Commonwealth usage.
