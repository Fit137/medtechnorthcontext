# Glossary

Every term used across this repo with a specific meaning. One line each. If a term is not here, do not use it in a document.

---

## Ambiguous words: do not use these alone

| Never write | Write instead |
|---|---|
| **"report"** | **Composition report** (who was in the room) or **insight brief** (research we sell). Two different documents, different buyers, different prices |
| **"the event"** | **Room** (our gathering) or **session** (one funded room) |
| **"sponsor"** | **Co-host**, **session partner**, or **funder**, depending on which one it is. Also banned in public entirely |
| **"partner"** | **Co-host**, **session partner**, **venue partner**, or **referral partner**. Say which |
| **"the deal"** | Name the product: co-hosted room, session, insight brief, market validation |

## The room and who is in it

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **The room** | A single MedTech North gathering. Also used for the network as a concept |
| **Session** | One room that someone paid for. A room is the format, a session is the transaction |
| **Composition** | The balance of groups in a room, counted by role. The actual product we sell |
| **The four groups** | Clinicians and researchers · builders (founders and operators) · capital and corporate · policy and government |
| **Builders** | Companies building health technology at any stage. Used instead of "startups", which excludes scale-ups |
| **Validators** | People who judge whether a thing works in practice: clinicians, practice owners, operations leaders. The other half of the room |
| **Guest** | Someone attending free: clinician, researcher, academic, student, public sector. Never a discount tier |
| **Service provider** | Agencies, consultants, law firms, recruiters, vendors selling into the sector. A paid segment, capped hard, never free |
| **The scarce side** | The groups who create the room's value and are never charged. Internal term only |
| **The magnet** | The free side, whose presence makes the paid side sellable. Internal only |
| **Chapter** | A city. Also the name of the entry paid membership tier |
| **Chapter ceiling** | Maximum paying members one city supports, around 35 at quarterly cadence |
| **Guest-to-payer ratio** | Guests per paying member. Target 1.5:1. Past 2:1, stop recruiting guests |
| **1:1 builders to validators** | Composition target. Half the seats build, half judge |
| **Founding cohort** | First 20 clinicians and first 40 builders. Status and locked rates. Closes once |

## What we sell

| Term | What it actually is | Price |
|---|---|---|
| **Co-hosted room** | We design, compose, produce and facilitate a room **inside the client's own offices**. They get co-host billing and a welcome. Primary commercial line | $25K to $45K |
| **Session** | A room we host, funded by one company. They get a welcome, one seat, a composition report | $3.5K to $7.5K |
| **Insight brief** | A written research document, 12 to 18 pages, built from clinician interviews and what rooms produce. Aggregated, anonymised, nobody named. **This is the thing we sell, not a summary of an event** | $12K to $25K commissioned, or $3.5K syndicated |
| **Market validation** | For a foreign company entering Canada: one composed room, written intelligence, a map of who matters, and consented introductions | $20K to $35K |
| **Advisory roundtable** | 8 to 15 clinicians give structured input on a client's question. Clinicians are paid honoraria here | $18K to $35K |
| **Convening as a service** | Umbrella name for co-hosted rooms and market validation. The category we are in | |
| **Commissioned** | One buyer pays full price and gets 90-day exclusivity on the insight brief | |
| **Syndicated** | Six to eight buyers each pay a smaller amount for the same insight brief. Faster cash | |

## How we price and close

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Make-good** | If we miss the written composition target, the next session is free. Removes the buyer's downside. The main reason an unproven property closes |
| **The ladder** | Order of products, cheapest first: observer seat, session, co-hosted room, category-exclusive, anchor. Never skip a rung uninvited |
| **Observer seat** | One free seat per room for a prospect. Same seat as everyone else, never a showcase |
| **Category-exclusive** | One company holds a vertical alone for a year. $10K to $25K |
| **Anchor** | Funds the core programme, woven into the identity. $25K to $60K a year |
| **The never list** | What we never **supply** to anyone: an attendee list, an export, or contact data. Also never sold: guest list before the room, stage time, editorial control, shared category, promised outcomes. We do not claim to stop people talking in a room |
| **Category exclusivity** | The funder's category is locked to them for that room. No direct competitor is admitted at any price. **The only thing exactly one company can hold, so the only thing that carries a premium** |
| **Conflict check** | We screen every commercial seat against the funder's category before confirming it, the way a law firm does. Refused seats are refunded |
| **Price tracks extraction** | Clinicians create the value and never pay. Everyone else pays in proportion to what they take out of the room |
| **Retained-rights clause** | Contract terms keeping the guest list, relationships, data and reinvitation rights with us. Without it we are doing work-for-hire |
| **Work-for-hire** | A deal where the client owns the guest list. Same margin, but the network gains nothing. Price higher or decline |
| **Vendor readiness pack** | The five things that turn a yes into cash: business name, liability insurance, incorporation decision, one-page agreement, composition report template |
| **The committee line** | Roughly $25K. Below it a manager signs. Above it, a committee and a budget cycle |
| **Partner activation** | The budget line a partnerships manager already owns. Easier to approve than "sponsorship" for the same money |

## Screening and targeting

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Firmographic screen** | The fixed list of company attributes we check before spending time on a prospect |
| **The three gates** | Gate 1: someone who owns the outcome can sign. Gate 2: no promotional regulatory review. Gate 3: the cheque is under the committee line |
| **Audience relevance** | Does this company sell to, hire from, or serve the people in the room? High is required |
| **Category stake** | Does this company have a commercial interest in what the room concludes? Low is required |
| **Topical orthogonality** | A co-host whose product has no stake in the room's subject. What made the Miro rooms work |
| **Orthogonal / adjacent / conflicted** | The three category-stake ratings. Orthogonal can co-host. Conflicted cannot, and buys an insight brief instead |
| **Trigger** | A dated event in the last 12 months that moved a company's market and creates urgency. Strongest is a new Canadian healthcare leader |
| **Installed base** | The customers a software vendor already has. What a partnerships buyer is really shopping for |
| **Borrowed distribution** | Reaching many customers through one partner's installed base instead of selling to each |
| **Tier A / B / C** | Priority bands in the target lists. A closes now, B in months three to six, C is opportunistic |
| **✅ / ⬜** | In data files: confirmed by dated research with a source, versus unverified and needs checking before contact |

## Rules and guardrails

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Buyer-led room design** | Designing a room backwards from what a named buyer's function is measured on |
| **The no-funder test** | State a room's topic and composition with no company attached. If it is not worth running, the funder is curating and the room is dead |
| **Hosting is fine, curating is not** | A company may host a room and shape its theme. They never touch the guest list |
| **Funder-content firewall** | The funder pays for content and holds no editorial control. Standard independent educational support structure |
| **Chatham House** | What is said may be used, who said it may not. Default in every room |
| **Partner-hosted** | A room in a donated venue. $55 a seat versus $105 in a restaurant. The biggest cost lever we have |
| **Sponsor attach rate** | Share of rooms with a funder or donated venue. Must reach 80% by year two |

## Formats

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Patient Journey Jam** | Founders map a patient journey live on a board, the room annotates. Run twice |
| **The front desk room** | Planned room on what happens at the front desk of dental practices and pharmacies since July 2026. An administrative Patient Journey Jam |
| **Second Opinion session** | Two or three vetted clinicians walk a founder's clinical journey live, 60 to 90 minutes |
| **The signature moment** | The composing table on the website. Forty seats sort into balance |
| **The question** | One structured question every dinner answers, identical across all five cities in a quarter. Without it a dinner has nothing to sell |
| **Chapter clinical lead** | One respected clinician per city who co-owns the room, brings the first twelve and names the venue. No lead, no city |
| **Refundable deposit** | $50 held and returned at the door. A commitment device, not a fee. Only added if a city exceeds 25% no-show |
| **Day of rooms** | Six to eight composed rooms in one venue on one day, each a different specialty with its own funder. Our answer to a summit. No room exceeds 35 |
| **Summit** | A single large conference with a stage and exhibitors. Not run in year one. See `ADR-018` |

## Outside terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **PAAB** | Pharmaceutical Advertising Advisory Board. Pre-clears promotional material aimed at health professionals. Adds 8 to 12 weeks. Drugs and vaccines only |
| **MLR** | Medical, legal, regulatory review. Internal to each company, happens every time, cannot be pre-cleared once |
| **CGL** | Commercial general liability insurance. The one item that cannot be skipped |
| **CDCP** | Canadian Dental Care Plan. 2026-27 benefit year opened 1 July 2026 |
| **Minor ailments** | Conditions Ontario pharmacists can assess and prescribe for. Went from 19 to 28 on 1 July 2026 |
| **DSO** | Dental service organisation. A group that owns or runs many practices |
| **Banner** | A brand that independent pharmacies operate under, such as Guardian, IDA or Pharmasave |
| **PMS** | Practice management software. What a dental or pharmacy front desk runs on |
| **ACV** | Annual contract value. What one customer pays a vendor per year |
| **ISV / embed partner** | A software vendor who builds another company's product into their own |
| **FDI** | Foreign direct investment. What Invest Ontario and Toronto Global are measured on |
