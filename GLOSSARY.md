# Glossary

Terms used across this repo with specific meanings.

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **The room** | A single MedTech North gathering. Also used for the network as a concept |
| **Composition** | The balance of groups in a room. The core product. Measured by role, never by headcount |
| **The four groups** | Clinicians and researchers · builders (founders and operators) · capital and corporate · policy and government |
| **Builders** | Companies building health technology, at any stage. Used in preference to "startups", which excludes scale-ups and established companies |
| **Guest** | Someone attending free: clinician, researcher, academic, student or public sector. Never a discount tier |
| **The scarce side** | The groups who create the room's value and are never charged. Internal term only, never public |
| **Chapter** | A city. Also the name of the entry paid membership tier |
| **Chapter ceiling** | The maximum paying members a city can support, ~35 at quarterly cadence |
| **Guest-to-payer ratio** | Guests per paying member. Target ~1.5:1. Past 2:1, pause guest recruitment |
| **1:1 builders to validators** | Composition target. Half the seats build, half validate |
| **Partner-hosted** | A room in a donated venue. $55/seat versus $105 restaurant. The biggest cost lever |
| **Sponsor attach rate** | Share of dinners with a sponsor or donated venue. Must reach 80% by year two |
| **The magnet** | The free side of the network, whose presence makes the paid side sellable. Internal only |
| **Composition report** | Post-event report describing the room by role, never by name. The proof asset |
| **Insight brief** | Written intelligence product sold to corporates. Aggregated, anonymised, nobody named |
| **Advisory roundtable** | Paid session where 8–15 clinicians give structured input. Clinicians receive honoraria |
| **Funder-content firewall** | Structure where a sponsor funds content but has no editorial control. Standard "independent educational support" |
| **The signature moment** | The composing table. Forty seats sort into balance. One interaction the visitor remembers |
| **Founding cohort** | First 20 clinicians and first 40 builders. Status and locked rates. Closes once |
| **Patient Journey Jam** | Event format: founders map a patient journey live on Miro, the room annotates |
| **Chatham House** | What is said may be used, who said it may not. Default in every room |
