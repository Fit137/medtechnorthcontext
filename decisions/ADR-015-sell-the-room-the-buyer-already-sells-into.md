# ADR-015: Sell the room the buyer already sells into, dental first

**Status:** Accepted

## Context
Corporate outreach has not started. `HANDOFF.md` records it as deliberately sequenced after proof. With one room held and the composition question resolved by `ADR-012`, the question of *which* companies to approach first is now live, and it needs to be settled before outreach begins rather than discovered through wasted calls.

## The question
Approach the buyers a health innovation network is expected to approach, meaning large pharmaceutical and device companies, or approach the companies whose own customers already sit in the room that assembled?

## The premise that was wrong
"The prestige buyer is the right first buyer." Prestige and winnability run in opposite directions here. The largest cheques sit behind a global signature, PAAB and full MLR review, and a vendor onboarding process that a business with no incorporation and no liability insurance cannot yet enter. The most winnable buyer is a Canadian company with a marketing manager holding discretionary spend, whose market changed on a dated trigger in 2026, and whose customer is a practice owner already in the room.

## The second premise that was wrong
"The fastest yes is the right yes." Professional services firms selling to founders score highest on speed and tie for highest on win probability, and they still fail, because the room contained two founders and service providers are capped for a reason. Composition safety carries a 0.20 weight specifically so this trade-off is decided once, in writing, rather than re-litigated whenever a cheque is on the table.

## Decision
1. **Dental is the first named specialty room.** Trigger is the CDCP 2026-27 benefit year and the April 2026 grid revision. Dentists own their practices, dental marketing is study-club native, and PAAB exposure is close to zero.
2. **Pharmacy runs second on a slower clock.** Its trigger is larger, and its biggest buyers are its slowest.
3. **The offer is always two lines in one conversation:** a specialty session at $3,500 to $7,500 from marketing budget, and a category brief at $3,500 syndicated or $12,000 to $25,000 commissioned from research budget. They draw on different budgets and clear different review paths, so they fail independently.
4. **Room two runs unsponsored as a named dental room**, with the outreach funnel instrumented. Per-specialty convening is currently a hypothesis, not a proof. Rooms three and four are sold against the resulting funnel table.
5. **Every commercial agreement states a composition target in writing and carries a make-good**, because that is what closes an unproven property. Composition may be promised. Commercial outcomes may never be.
6. **The vendor readiness pack is week-one work**: business name, CGL insurance, incorporation decision, invoice template, one-page agreement, composition report template. A verbal yes is not cash until these exist.

## What this does not authorise
No change to public language. `ADR-007` stands: none of this vocabulary appears on the site or anywhere a member reads. No sale of attendee data, stage time, editorial control, shared category or guest-list visibility, at any price. No claim of a track record beyond one room of sixteen people. No company named in `data/corporate-targets.md` may be described anywhere as a partner, because none is.
