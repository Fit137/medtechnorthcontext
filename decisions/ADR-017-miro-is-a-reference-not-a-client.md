# ADR-017: Miro is a reference, not a future client

**Status:** Accepted

## Context
`ADR-016` recorded that both rooms held to date were commissioned by Miro, and named a Miro expansion conversation as the warmest five-figure opportunity available. That recommendation is withdrawn on the founder's own account of the relationship, which carries information the repo did not have.

## Why the expansion does not exist
**Healthcare is not Miro's ICP.** A healthcare use case was found and professors were brought in to present using the product, but that was the founder solving Miro's problem creatively, not Miro asking for healthcare. Building a long-term format around a vertical the client never requested puts the work on a foundation the client has no reason to fund.

**The budget is already spent.** Miro allocates a maximum annual amount to this kind of engagement and it has been fully drawn. Further Miro-branded rooms would be unpaid work carrying a client's name.

## Decision
Miro is treated as a **completed engagement and a live reference**. No further Miro rooms are pursued. All convening from here is MedTech North's own, run for co-hosts who pay.

The reference is the asset that survives, and it is worth more than another Miro room would have been: a global software company commissioned convening twice and endorsed the output publicly. That is the proof, and proof travels to buyers who have healthcare in their ICP.

## What this changes in the target profile
The Miro engagement worked despite a poor firmographic fit, carried by the relationship. Repeating it means finding companies with **Miro's neutrality and a healthcare ICP**, which is a specific and searchable combination rather than a lucky one.

The two-axis screen in `data/cohost-targets.md` follows directly. High audience relevance, low category stake. Miro sat in the low-relevance quadrant, which is exactly why the budget had a ceiling. A company selling cloud, data, security or professional services into Canadian healthcare sits in the high-relevance quadrant with the same neutrality, and its budget grows with the vertical rather than capping out.

## Still to do on the relationship
Ask Miro for written permission to name them as a reference, ideally with a two-line quote. This is a closing act on a finished engagement rather than an opening for a new one, and it should be framed that way. Without permission the name may not be used in any material, per `CLAUDE.md` rule 3 and `data/verified-stats.md`.

## What this does not change
`ADR-016` stands in every other respect. Co-hosted convening remains the primary commercial line, at the same prices, with the same topical-orthogonality screen and the same retained-rights clause.
