# ADR-016: Co-hosted convening is the primary commercial line, not sponsorship

**Status:** Accepted

## Context
The repo recorded one room held and nothing sold. Both were understated. **Two rooms have been held and both were commissioned by Miro**, who engaged the founder to convene attendees and run workshops on the Miro product. Miro's official brand account endorsed the output publicly. Four attendees posted public, attributable testimonials on LinkedIn under their own names, including a professor at McMaster University. Outreach across both rooms produced a self-reported ~70% positive reply rate, with volume deliberately constrained by the condo's capacity rather than by demand.

Convening as a service is therefore not a hypothesis. It is a delivered service with a paying global software client and a public endorsement, which had not been recorded anywhere.

## The question
Continue building toward per-event sponsorship at $3,000 to $7,500, or lead with the service that has already been sold at a higher price to a better buyer?

## Decision
**Co-hosted convening at the client's own headquarters becomes the primary commercial line.** Per-event sponsorship drops to a secondary product for buyers who cannot host. The category brief remains the fastest cash and attaches to everything.

Pricing: $25,000 to $45,000 per co-hosted room, $90,000 to $150,000 for a four-room annual programme. Above the bands in `docs/01-business-model.md`, deliberately, because this is a produced service with a proven delivery and a marquee reference rather than an event sponsorship.

**A second line is opened in parallel:** Canadian market validation for foreign companies with no Canadian presence, at $20,000 to $35,000 entry and $60,000 to $120,000 for a programme. Slower, no natural trigger, best pricing power in the business.

## The premise that was wrong
"Sponsor" was the wrong noun. A sponsor buys attention and is therefore priced against a trade show booth. A co-host buys a produced outcome and is priced against what producing it internally costs them, which is a larger number they already know. The word also moves the purchase into field marketing and events budgets, which are bigger and more routinely spent, and it survives compliance review more comfortably because editorial independence in a genuine co-production is a recognisable structure.

## The second premise that was wrong
`docs/11-corporate-outreach.md` gate 1 required a Canadian profit and loss statement behind the signature. Correct for a small marketing sponsorship, wrong as a general rule. For a market entry purchase the global team is the natural owner, and having no Canadian division **removes** an approval layer rather than adding one. Gate 1 is restated: the cheque must be signable by someone who owns the outcome being sold.

## The new rule this creates: topical orthogonality
A co-host's product must be orthogonal to the room's subject. Miro worked partly because a collaborative whiteboard has no stake in a clinical conclusion. A dental software vendor co-hosting a room about dental software does have a stake, and clinicians read that inside one evening. Category-adjacent companies buy the brief, a session, or an advisory roundtable where the interest is declared and bounded. They do not co-host.

## The clause that keeps this from becoming an agency
MedTech North retains the guest list, the relationships, the graph data, the intelligence and the right to reinvite every attendee. The client receives the room, the co-host position, a welcome rather than a keynote, the composition report, media assets and a written summary. **A client that contracts to own the attendee list is buying work-for-hire.** Identical margin, zero compounding. Price that version higher or decline it.

## Consequences
- `ADR-013` is reinforced rather than replaced. Client premises were already identified as the answer to the venue problem. This makes them the default rather than the exception.
- `docs/06-financial-model.md` partner-hosted share moves toward 100% for paid rooms, which is the bull case structurally rather than optimistically.
- The condo amenity keeps its role from `ADR-013`: free ungated rooms, interviews, recorded panels. Nothing paid happens there.
- `ADR-011` stands. The brief is still the fastest cash and now attaches to both new lines.
- The first call is Miro, to expand a live account rather than open a cold one.

## What this does not authorise
No change to public language. `ADR-007` stands: none of this vocabulary appears anywhere a member reads. Miro may not be named as a reference, and no testimonial may be quoted in any material, until written permission is held. No claim of a track record beyond two rooms. No promised commercial outcome, per `docs/08-legal-and-compliance.md`.
