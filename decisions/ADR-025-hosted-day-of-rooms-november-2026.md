# ADR-025: The November scale-up is a hosted day of rooms, and the three asks are split

**Status:** Accepted
**Date:** 2026-08-21
**Extends:** `ADR-018` (no summit in year one), `ADR-013` (venue split), `ADR-021` (venue is a discount)

## Context

The ambition changed. The stated goal is a large event in Canada before December 2026, national reach rather than GTA only, and a US inbound line, with cold outreach scaled hard against all three. The founder is willing to travel anywhere in Canada and does not want to commit personal cash to a venue with AV and a stage and then sell against a fixed date.

`ADR-018` already refused a summit in year one, for reasons that have not changed: it attacks our own position, our own scoring rejected large promotional formats, the vocabulary is banned in public, and roughly $50,000 to $70,000 of venue, AV and catering minimums are committed before a ticket sells. Free in-person events run 40 to 60% no-show, so the free half of the room carries the worst attendance economics available, and a thin room in front of paying commercial guests is worse than no room.

**Nothing in the new ambition weakens that arithmetic. What changes it is the venue being obtained rather than bought.**

## Decision

**Run a day of rooms inside a hosted building on Thursday 12 November 2026, with Thursday 5 November as the alternate.**

Six to eight composed rooms in one venue on one day, each on a different question, each with its own funder where one exists. **No room over 35.** Attendance across the day of 150 to 250 is a consequence, never a promise and never a published figure.

**The three asks are split and run as separate campaigns at separate speeds.**

| Ask | Given | Received | Time to yes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Venue host** | The building, its AV, its staff for the day | Co-branding, presence, media shot in their building, category held, two nominated seats | 2 to 4 weeks |
| **Room funder** | $7,500 to $25,000 | A composed room to their brief, category held, composition report, introductions | 4 to 8 weeks |
| **Co-host** | Money and premises | Both, plus the co-host position | 8 to 16 weeks |

Bundling them means the slowest sets the date. The venue ask closes fastest because nothing is bought, so nothing clears a procurement threshold.

**The city is an output of the campaign, not an input.** The host campaign runs in every corridor at once and the first qualified yes that also clears the clinician gate decides where we fly. The GTA remains the highest prior and a non-GTA yes has to clear a higher bar, because there is no list anywhere else.

**The scarce-side gate, and it is hard.** No host agreement is signed in any city until at least 12 practising clinicians or practice owners in that city have replied positively. A donated building is worthless if the room seats nine, and that failure is public, unrecoverable and paid for in airfare.

## What is not committed before a signed host

No venue deposit, no catering minimum, no AV hire, no fixed cost that survives a cancellation. **Hard gate: a host agreement signed by 30 September 2026.** If it is not signed by then, the day of rooms moves to Q1 2027 and November becomes a single composed room in the GTA. That decision gets made on the day rather than allowed to drift, because drift is how the cash risk in `ADR-018` arrives through the back door.

## The consideration offered to a host is media, and that is deliberate

`docs/19-host-incentives-and-the-media-floor.md` carries the argument. In short: media is the only deliverable entirely inside our control on the night, it is legally clean where a promised outcome is not, and the scarce input is the participants rather than the production. Any organisation can book a crew. None of them can book twelve practising clinicians willing to sit on camera, because those people will not do it for a vendor and every one of them has been asked.

**This creates a dependency that is now on the critical path.** There is no sample reel. Room 003 on 26 August 2026 is the asset shoot for the host campaign as well as an event, and a two minute reel plus vertical cuts must exist by 2 September or campaign 1 stays at low volume.

## Why this is not `ADR-018` being reversed

| | Summit, refused | Hosted day of rooms |
|---|---|---|
| Fixed cost before revenue | $50,000 to $70,000 | **Zero.** The building is donated and nothing else is committed before a funder exists |
| Downside if it underfills | Costs already spent | Fewer rooms run. The day still happens |
| Room size | Hundreds | **None over 35** |
| Exhibitors and booths | Required | None. A funder composes a room rather than renting a table |
| Stage | The product | Facilitators inside rooms. No keynote |
| Contradiction with the moat | Direct | None |

The five conditions `ADR-018` set before a real summit is reconsidered are unchanged and unmet. This is not a summit and must never be described as one, in public or in private.

## Consequences

- `data/venue-host-targets.md` is created as a list distinct from `data/cohost-targets.md`. Different screen, different archetypes, different ranking. The two overlap less than expected
- Insurance and business name registration move onto the critical path for the date, not only for invoicing. A facilities team asks for a certificate of insurance before it gives a date
- `docs/16-product-architecture.md` pricing is unchanged. A room inside a shared day is a room, priced as one
- `ADR-020` applies per room, not per day. Category is held for the room a funder buys
- `ADR-021` still holds: the host supplying the building lowers cost, it does not buy anything the never list forbids

## What this does not authorise

No public use of summit, sponsor, exhibitor, booth, partner tier or media rights, per rule 2 and `ADR-007`. No attendance figure published before or after, per rule 3. No claim of physician attendance. No named reference or quoted testimonial until written permission is held, per `ADR-017`. No room over 35 for any amount of money. No paid venue.
