# ADR-013 — Free venue for unsponsored rooms, client venues for paid

**Status:** Accepted

## Decision
The condo amenity hosts free, ungated rooms only. Anything a sponsor pays for happens at the sponsor's own office or an external venue.

## Why
Two problems solved at once. The condo declaration is assumed to prohibit commercial use, and gating access on paid subscription would be the clearest possible evidence of it. Separately, the venue actively repels physicians and anyone making a legitimacy judgment.

## The unlock
**The client's own office** for paid advisory roundtables. Costs nothing, removes the commercial-use question entirely, gives them home-field comfort, and the guest list still belongs to MedTech North.

## Best use of the amenity
| Amenity | Use |
|---|---|
| Sofas | Recorded 2–3 person panels. Zero cost, produces sellable inventory |
| Boardroom (8) | One-on-one clinician interviews, any weekday |
| Main lounge | The monthly core room, 25 to 35 |

## Capacity decision
Work at 25 to 35, not the permitted 50. Fifty in a residential building with hired security is the configuration that generates a complaint, and composition quality drops as size rises.

## Also decided
Venue partnerships are worth more than any price rise. Moving from all-restaurant unsponsored to all-partner-hosted with a $4,000 sponsor is a ~$148,000 annual swing on the dinner programme. The organisations with free downtown boardrooms are exactly the category-partner verticals, so recruiting venues *is* recruiting sponsors.
