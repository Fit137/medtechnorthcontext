# ADR-001 — Hybrid model

**Status:** Accepted

## Decision
Combine low-ticket paid membership (founders, operators, companies) with high-ticket corporate revenue (insight briefs, advisory roundtables, sponsorship).

## Alternatives
**Pure sponsorship** monetises attention, but a free-RSVP audience is cheap, unverified inventory with high no-show rates. Sponsors pay less because the room is not provably curated.

**Pure membership** has a hard cold start, since nobody pays dues into an unproven community, and revenue is capped by member count alone.

## Why
The subscription buys the proof that makes corporate revenue premium, and corporate revenue funds a programme good enough to make the subscription worth paying for. Each stream makes the other more valuable.

## Consequence
Membership is deliberately under 30% of revenue. It exists as a qualification mechanism, not an income line. Anyone reading the financial model should not treat low membership revenue as underperformance.
