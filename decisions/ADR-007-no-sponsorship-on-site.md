# ADR-007 — Zero sponsorship content on the public website

**Status:** Accepted. Non-negotiable.

## Decision
No sponsorship or monetisation content anywhere public. Not a section, sentence, nav item, footer link, FAQ answer or metadata string.

## The trigger
A site section headed "Sponsor the composition, not the crowd" closed with: *"You are not sponsoring access to people who wandered in. You are sponsoring access to people who were chosen."*

A clinician reading that sees themselves described as the thing being sold. No surrounding copy recovers from it.

## Forbidden terms in public copy
sponsor · sponsorship · partner tier · anchor · category-exclusive · dinner partner · summit partner · exhibitor · underwriter · booth · media rights · lead access · any sentence describing how the organisation earns money

## Where it goes instead
Private documents shared on calls, in outreach campaigns, and in data rooms. A `/partners` route may exist for outreach links but is removed from navigation and carries `noindex`.

## One deliberate exception
The "nobody buys a microphone" rule stays in public copy. It is sponsorship-adjacent but works in the reader's favour, telling a clinician that even paying parties do not get to pitch them.
