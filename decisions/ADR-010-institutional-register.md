# ADR-010 — Institutional motion register

**Status:** Accepted

## Decision
The site's motion register is **Institutional**: objects hold, entry 300 to 500 ms, restraint is the message. Not Announcement, despite the request for 3D and liveness.

## Why
The audience is senior clinicians and public sector leaders who make legitimacy judgments in seconds. A confirmed physician speaker withdrew from the first event within seconds of learning the venue was residential.

For this audience, restraint reads as serious and spectacle reads as a pitch.

## How liveness is still achieved
By spending the entire spectacle budget on **one signature moment** where the motion is the argument rather than decoration: the composing table, where forty seats arrive unsorted and physically sort into balance.

Effort budget: 40% on the signature moment, 40% on everything else, 20% on states and edges.

## Consequence
At least two surfaces carry explicit restraint notes saying where not to animate. The rules section gets one hover state and no entry animation, because rules that move read as negotiable.
