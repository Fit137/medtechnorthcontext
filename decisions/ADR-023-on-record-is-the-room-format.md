# ADR-023 — On Record is the room format, with two-tier capture

**Status:** Accepted

## Context

Rooms 001 and 002 were commissioned by Miro and ran the Miro product as the theme. Room 003 carries no co-host and needs a format that earns attendance on its own.

Three formats built around clinician participation were proposed and rejected: founders presenting to be scored, a ranked friction list, and a policy discussion. All three asked the scarcest person in the room to do work and gave the value to somebody else. For an owner-operator running a six or seven-figure practice, who is already asked for the same evening by every vendor in the sector, that invitation is deleted rather than declined.

## Decision

**The room is On Record.** Practice owners are professionally photographed and filmed talking about their own work, and they leave owning the material outright, free, with no obligation.

**The scarce side receives. The abundant side works and pays.** Founders apply, pay, get no stage time, no deck and no demo, and earn a consented introduction after the room rather than a conversation during it.

## The premise that was wrong

That a room's value to a clinician is the content of the room. For an owner-operator it is not. The value has to be an object with a price on it, delivered with no task attached, or the evening does not clear the cost of their time.

## Capture is split into two tiers

Twenty minutes per owner does not fit an evening. Twenty owners at twenty minutes is 6 hours 40 minutes against a usable capture window of roughly 105 minutes, and the dinner has to keep running throughout.

| Tier | Who | Where | Length |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Portrait and Question** | Everyone | On the night, one lit station running alongside dinner | ~7 minutes per owner |
| **The Feature** | Whoever books one | At their own practice, on another day | 45 to 60 minutes |

The deep interview moves off the event because a dinner venue produces generic footage regardless of how long it runs. Footage shot in the owner's own practice is better material and a second unhurried touchpoint with a qualified person.

**The on-camera question is identical for every owner in every room.** It removes preparation, makes editing consistent, and produces a series that compounds. Varying it produces interviews that do not.

## What was explicitly not chosen

**Interviewing only a few owners on the night.** It splits the room into featured and attending, makes the unfeatured evening a plain dinner, and ranks guests, which `CLAUDE.md` rule 6 forbids and which is unrecoverable once noticed.

## Constraints carried forward

- Owners pay nothing **only in a co-host funded room** (`ADR-026`, superseding `ADR-002`). Outside one, they buy a ticket like everyone else. They are never pitched on the night either way
- Owners own their footage outright. Any use by MedTech North needs separate written permission, and site publication needs its own ADR (`CLAUDE.md` rule 3)
- No patient information on camera, in any frame
- Professional advertising rules per college must be checked before the first shoot. No superlatives or comparative claims in any edit, and every interviewee approves their cut
- Publicly, a paying company is a **co-host**, never a sponsor (`ADR-007`)

## Commercial consequence

A produced room carries a real cost per event and therefore sits at the **$7,500 session partner** line rather than the $2,500 meetup line in `docs/16-product-architecture.md`. The co-host funds a production and receives footage and a composition report, never the attendee list.

## Open, and deliberately not decided here

Whether the ICP is now formally owner-operators rather than clinicians broadly. That changes `docs/03-icp-and-segments.md` and needs its own ADR once room 003 has run.
