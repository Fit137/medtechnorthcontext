# ADR-008 — Widen the rules to protect all groups equally

**Status:** Accepted

## Decision
Change "Clinicians are guests, never prospects" to **"No one here is anyone's prospect."**

## Why
The original read to founders and corporates as: *you are the thing we are guarding against.* Every ICP reads the same site.

The wider version protects clinicians from pitching, founders from service providers selling to them, and corporates from being treated as a chequebook. Same rule, three beneficiaries, and more accurate.

## Same treatment applied to
The attendee-data rule, which originally implied corporates were the risk. Rewritten to protect everyone in the room.

## The general principle
Any rule that names one group as protected implicitly names another as the threat. Write rules to cover the room, not to police a segment.
