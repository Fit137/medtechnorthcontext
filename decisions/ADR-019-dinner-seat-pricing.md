# ADR-019: Seat fees cover food, the funder cheque is the profit

**Status:** Accepted

## Decision
Per dinner of 24 seats: 12 clinicians and researchers free, 7 builders at $150, 4 service providers at $350, 1 funder seat included in their fee.

Seat fees cover the food. The funder fee is the margin.

## Clinicians stay free, for a reason beyond the existing rule
`ADR-002` says charging the scarce side collapses the asset. True, and there is a sharper reason. Clinicians pay for **credentials and education**: CME, certification, association membership, study clubs. MedTech North offers neither and is forbidden from implying either by rule 7. Charging them asks them to buy something we do not sell.

## Rejected: a $20 to $25 clinician fee
Too small to change behaviour, and it converts an invitation into a transaction. It also prices the dinner as a meetup. A dinner by nomination has no price at all.

## Rejected: 1:1 clinician to service provider at cost
Twelve service providers in a room of 24 is a vendor floor. The first event turned service providers away and that was correct. Break-even also pays for food and pays the founder nothing.

**Instead: cap service providers at 4 and charge $350.** Fewer seats, higher price, same revenue, no damage to composition. The price is also a qualification filter.

## The suspicion problem, which the rules did not previously address
A free dinner invitation from an unknown party reads as "you are the product." The answer is not a price, it is the invitation: named nomination, a stated seat count, an assigned neighbour, a role at the table, and a waitlist mentioned once. Being invited is the opposite of being the product.

## No-show
Target under 20%. Devices are free: assigned seat with a named neighbour, a role, personal confirmation 48 hours out, a visible waitlist, and overbooking by 20%.

**If a city exceeds 25% no-show after two dinners, add a $50 refundable deposit returned at the door.** Refundable deposits lift attendance 15 to 20%. It does not breach rule 1 because the money is returned. Do not add friction before the data requires it.

## What makes a dinner sellable at all
**Every dinner carries one structured question, the same question in all five cities in the same quarter.** Without it there is no content, and a funder is buying a meal. With it, the five cities produce a national read on one question, which is an insight brief at $12,000 to $25,000 rather than $3,500.

The dinners are the instrument. The insight brief is the product.
