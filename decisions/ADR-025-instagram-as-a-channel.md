# ADR-025 — Instagram as a channel, and the four rules it changes

**Status:** Proposed. Three items need Ali's sign-off before the first post, listed at the bottom.

## Context

MedTech North has published on LinkedIn and on Luma. `assets/social/launch-campaign.md` describes twelve static frames as a "launch social campaign" and names Instagram as a target surface. That kit is a LinkedIn kit. Running it on Instagram would spend a month producing the format the platform distributes least.

Three growth playbooks were reviewed and they agree on the mechanics: one niche, reels carrying roughly 80% of early effort, a repeatable series, the hook carrying 80% of the outcome, direct message automation rather than link-in-bio, and a structured format test before scaling. They also agree on two things that collide with this repo's rules, and the collisions are the reason this needs a decision rather than a plan.

## Decision

**Open the account, on a single niche, reels first.**

The niche is **convening**: what happens when clinicians, founders, researchers and policy people sit at one table, and what it takes to run those rooms. Not medtech, not Canadian health innovation, not clinicians. Those are the room's subject matter, and an account about them competes with every health publication in the country while saying nothing only this business can say.

Full plan in `assets/social/instagram-strategy-and-calendar.md`. Build brief in `prompts/claude-design/instagram-visual-system.md`.

## The four things this changes

### 1. The founder's face becomes part of the public brand

The institutional register has held partly because no person stood in front of it. There is no version of Instagram growth that survives that, and the source material is direct about faces being the strongest visual hook available.

Precedent exists and is narrow: `assets/website/website-design-concepts.md` places a founder portrait on the About page. This extends it from one image to a recurring format.

**The boundary that keeps rule 3 intact:** Ali's face is the founder's face. No guest appears in any frame, no populated table is depicted, and no image implies attendance. The only faces that ever join his are guests filmed under On Record, who own their footage outright and have given written permission.

### 2. Entertainment becomes a stated objective, and it means disclosure

Meta's own position, as relayed in the source material, is that entertainment has moved from a consideration to the primary driving factor in whether content is distributed. `docs/07-brand-and-voice.md` sets the register as executive, written as to a hospital chief of staff.

These reconcile, and the reconciliation is worth writing down because it will be tested every time a post is drafted.

**Entertainment here is disclosure, not comedy.** Showing the mechanics of something people only ever see the outside of, and admitting the parts that went badly. The agenda that was cut. The best line on the page that was pulled because it could not be kept. The room turning out to be dentists and pharmacists rather than the physicians it was built for.

No stunt, no spectacle, no challenge format. The source material's own advice is not to chase the extreme examples, and for an audience that makes legitimacy judgements in seconds the trade is plainly bad.

### 3. Direct message automation replaces link-in-bio

Comment a keyword, receive the link. It is the mechanism the source material rates highest for converting attention into revenue, and it fits this business unusually well, because the funnel is gated by application and nomination rather than by a checkout.

**Three constraints on it.**

The nomination keyword automates opening the thread and nothing further. `docs/10-growth-flywheel.md` is explicit that the nomination mechanic dies the moment it reads as a growth tactic, so the reply routes to Ali and the judgement stays human.

The wording carries no urgency device. "Comment TABLE and the link comes to you" states a mechanism. "Comment below and I'll send it" sells one.

**CASL has not been checked and is a genuine open item.** A keyword reply is solicited and sits on solid ground. The automatic message on a new follow is unsolicited, and although the drafted version contains no offer, it is the kind of thing this audience notices. See `docs/08-legal-and-compliance.md`.

### 4. The emoji ban holds

`docs/07-brand-and-voice.md` bans emoji absolutely. On Instagram that is a real departure from platform norms and it will make the account read as more formal than everything around it.

**Held, deliberately, and recorded as a cost rather than an oversight.** The audience makes legitimacy judgements in seconds and a clinician deciding whether this is a serious room is the reader who matters. Formality is the correct failure mode here.

## What this does not change

Nothing in `CLAUDE.md`. No headcount, no seat count, no attendance claim, no testimonial, no client name, no sponsorship term, no figure absent from `data/verified-stats.md`, no regulated title as a label, no segment ranked above another.

**One addition to the never list, specific to this platform.** A guest is never tagged into a post about a room they attended without asking first. A tag discloses their attendance to their entire network, which is the Chatham House rule broken by a user-interface convenience.

## The series, and why it is the whole thesis

**"What do patients get wrong about your field?"** Same question, every guest, every room, numbered publicly.

This is not invented for a content calendar. `docs/17-event-formats.md` Part 3 already specifies one fixed question for everyone, and gives the reason: twenty people answering one question is a format, one person answering twenty is an interview.

Two existing findings make it the strongest asset available to this channel. `docs/17` line 129: On Record is the only format producing public material that does not break the no-track-record rule, because the material is about the guest rather than about MedTech North. `docs/10-growth-flywheel.md` line 42: do not ask guests to share your content, manufacture the artifact they want to share.

Together those mean the series is not content that needs distribution. **It is the distribution.** Every guest posting their own clip carries the room into a professional network that could not otherwise be reached, and no ask was made.

## What this supersedes

`assets/social/launch-campaign.md` is superseded as an Instagram plan. Its master format, 1080 × 1350, survives. Its strategy does not, and several of its frames render a forty-seat table and use partner tier language, both of which are now forbidden. It is not deleted, because the LinkedIn copy in it remains useful, but it is not a starting point for this channel.
