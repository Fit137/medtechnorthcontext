# ADR-004 — Toronto-anchored positioning

**Status:** Accepted

## Decision
Keep "Toronto-anchored, Canada-wide" as public positioning, even though operations run from Mississauga.

## Alternative considered
Lead publicly with Mississauga and Peel. Genuinely stronger in some ways: nobody convenes Peel, Trillium Health Partners is one of Ontario's largest hospital networks and is local, and downtown Toronto is crowded with MaRS and OBIO.

## Why Toronto
Recognition with US companies and national sponsors who do not distinguish Peel from Durham. Those are the buyers in the highest-value revenue lines. Mississauga is legitimately GTA, so the claim is honest.

## Resolution
Brand reads national, operations read honest. The chapter list and individual event pages carry the specific city. Location in public listings is "Mississauga" or "Square One District", never the building.

## Chapters
Toronto, Ottawa, Montreal, Calgary, Vancouver. **Halifax removed** from earlier drafts.
