# ADR-014 — Status roles, not referral rewards

**Status:** Accepted

## Decision
No referral incentives, rewards, credits or perks in exchange for nominations. Growth runs on status roles instead.

## Why
A nomination is not a favour to MedTech North. "Who should be at this table?" is a question about someone's judgement, and being asked it is flattering. Attaching a reward converts a status act into a transaction and makes free membership feel like it had strings.

**Counterintuitively, the more it is incentivised the less it works.**

## Scoring
Referral rewards scored **4.75**, last of eight mechanics. The top four are all status roles: panel and cohort moderation (8.60), chapter clinical lead (8.50), institutional host session (8.30), nomination rights (8.10).

## The mechanic that replaces it
Give guests a role that raises their own standing. That role is visible to their network without any ask. The role naturally raises the question of who else belongs.

**Do not ask them to share content. Manufacture the artifact they want to share.** A recorded panel with their name on it gets posted by them and tagged by their institution.

## The wording
Never "refer a colleague". Always **"Who should be at this table?"** asked open, because a clinician's network contains device reps, spinout founders, industry partners and procurement leads, not only clinicians.

High-value variant: **"Which company should be in this room?"** A clinician naming a company is a warm path to a paying member.

## Volume guardrail
One or two nominations per person per year. Always personal, never automated, never a form email. **Reject nominations that do not fit.** A nomination that is always accepted is not an honour.
