How tonight runs

Three questions. Five minutes. Everyone speaks.

Order is alphabetical, by first name. The questions sit on the slide behind each speaker, so nobody has to carry them.

One mark per minute. When the last one fills red, finish the sentence you are on.
The room

Four rules we do not bend.

Nobody buys a microphone.
Every speaker gets the same five minutes and the same slide. No one paid for either.
No one here is anyone's prospect.
No ask from the stage: no raise, no pricing, no demo.
Nothing leaves the table.
What is said after the five-minute slots stays in this room.
Composition over headcount.
We will say who was here by role. Never how many.
The five-minute slots are on the record and may be photographed. The rest of the evening is not. Say so and you will not be recorded.
