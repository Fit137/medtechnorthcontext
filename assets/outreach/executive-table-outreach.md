# Executive Table Outreach: the message

> **INTERNAL.** Copy for working `data/prospects/executive-table-targets.csv`. Room context: `docs/17-who-belongs-in-the-room.md` (option F).
>
> No pricing, no tier language, no commercial vocabulary (`CLAUDE.md` rule 2). No headcount promise, no track record claim (rule 3).

---

## Step 1. Ask for permission to use the name

Worth several times the role line with this list, because these people know who he is. It costs him nothing, which is why it converts.

```
Duncan, quick one. I'm filling the last seats. May I say you're attending
when I invite others? Happy to keep it to "a health data CEO" if you'd rather.
```

---

## Step 2. The message

### With permission

```
[First name], I host a small table in Mississauga. Eight seats, no stage,
nobody pitching.

Duncan Weatherston, CEO of Smile Digital Health, is confirmed, with a health
sciences professor and a community pharmacist.

[Date]. Want a seat?
```

### Without permission, and until it is held in writing

```
[First name], I host a small table in Mississauga. Eight seats, no stage,
nobody pitching.

Confirmed: the CEO of a health data platform, a health sciences professor,
a community pharmacist.

[Date]. Want a seat?
```

Both fit a LinkedIn connection note. Where there is room, add one verified line about their own work above the opening line. Never flattery, one fact.

### Follow-up, day 5 or 6. Never a third.

```
[First name], closing the table this week. Keep you in mind for the next one?
```

### On a decline

```
Understood. Anyone you'd put in the room instead?
```

---

## Sending

Six today, six on day 4, the rest on day 8. Stop at four acceptances. Twenty-eight invitations against four seats means turning senior people away after they said yes, which costs more than a slower fill.

## Never in these messages

The name without written permission. Any price, tier or sponsorship word. "Eight executives" or any composition promise. Member counts or past-event claims. Urgency devices. Dashes or exclamation marks.
