# Reply: clinician asks "what is your business, and what do I get?"

Context: warm outbound to an individual clinician (allied health, private practice) who
answered an invitation to be listed in the member directory and asked what MedTech North
actually is. She reads as a practitioner, not as a founder. Answer in her scope only.

---

## Send-ready version

> Happy to keep it short.
>
> MedTech North is a network for the people who build health technology in Canada and the
> people who use it on patients. Most companies here design a device or an app for a year
> and a half, then meet their first clinician at the point of sale, when nothing can be
> changed anymore. I put those two groups in the same room earlier than that.
>
> On the directory, you would be listed by discipline, name and practice. That is how the
> network gets searched. When a founder or a researcher is working on something that
> touches your patients, they find you there and ask what would break in a real clinic day.
>
> What that gives you:
>
> - Your name in front of the people building the tools you will be handed in three years,
>   at the stage where what you say still changes them
> - An invitation to the rooms when composition calls for your discipline. No fee, in any
>   year, permanently. The clinical side never pays
> - The ability to nominate colleagues you would want at the table
> - Nobody sells to you. No pitching in either direction, and nothing said in the room
>   leaves it
>
> Two honest notes. This is not a patient-facing listing and it will not send you patients.
> And we are early: one room has been held, in the Toronto area, so being listed now means
> being findable before the network fills in.

## Shorter variant, if the thread is moving fast

> Short version: companies building health technology in Canada usually meet their first
> clinician at the point of sale, eighteen months after the design was frozen. I get them
> in front of clinicians before that.
>
> Being on the directory means founders and researchers can find you by discipline and ask
> what breaks in a real clinic day, and you get invited to the rooms when your discipline
> is called for. No fee, ever, for the clinical side. Nobody pitches you. It will not send
> you patients, that is not what it is for.

---

## Guardrails used

- Clinical side is free permanently. Never framed as a discount, a trial or a launch offer.
- No member counts, no testimonials, no implied roster. One room, 16 people, stated plainly
  or not at all.
- No sponsorship, partner tier or exhibitor language anywhere in a member-facing message.
- No regulated titles used as labels.
- No claim of patient referrals, practice growth, credit, certification or endorsement.
- Rooms have run in the Toronto area only. Do not imply a room in her city.
- No em dashes, no exclamation marks, no urgency.
