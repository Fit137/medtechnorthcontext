## Competitive advantage, in four words

**Composed rooms, guaranteed proximity.**

Every organisation in the set connects this ecosystem through programs, projects or scale events. **None composes a standing, balanced, intimate room and guarantees who is in it.**

The infrastructure players (MaRS, OBIO, CAN Health, MEDTEQ+) have mandates that compromise neutrality: they hold equity stakes, procurement roles or policy mandates. The events players (MedTech World, Medtech Canada) have scale that compromises curation.

MedTech North occupies the one position both structurally cannot: **small, balanced, neutral and provable.**

## Partnership, not just competition

**Delivery contracts with already-funded organisations scored 7.85** in the monetization analysis, the highest near-term revenue path outside the corporate products.

OBIO, CAN Health, MEDTEQ+ and MaRS hold budgets and reporting obligations and lack clinician convening capability. This is fee-for-service to a for-profit vendor, not a grant, so it remains available despite the decision to stay for-profit. **$15–60K per engagement, 3–6 months to first cash.**

**Also relevant:** RIC Centre (Mississauga, Brampton, Caledon innovation hub) and Toronto Global / Invest Ontario, whose FDI KPIs the US market-entry product directly serves.
