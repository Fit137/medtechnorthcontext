# ADR-003 — Stay for-profit

**Status:** Accepted

## Decision
Remain a for-profit entity. Do not incorporate a not-for-profit or pursue the ecosystem grant path.

## Context
Research showed nearly every Canadian ecosystem and convening fund requires a not-for-profit applicant. FedDev Ontario's Regional Innovation Ecosystem stream funds not-for-profit organisations, with contributions historically $500K–$10M at up to 50% of costs. Year-one budget here is ~$200K, an order of magnitude below their threshold.

Timeline to first grant dollar was assessed at **24 to 30 months** from incorporation, and contributions are reimbursement-based, requiring $60–90K of permanent working capital float.

## Why for-profit
Speed and founder economics. The commercial products (insight briefs, advisory roundtables) can generate revenue in months rather than years, and the business is self-funding within a year at modest volumes.

## What remains available
**Delivery contracts with already-funded organisations** (OBIO, CAN Health, MEDTEQ+, MaRS) are fee-for-service to a vendor, not grants. Scored 7.85, $15–60K per engagement, 3–6 months to cash. Municipal economic development partnerships and Invest Ontario / Toronto Global referral relationships also remain open.

## Reconsider if
Two years of track record accumulate, the mission grows genuinely bigger than one operator, and a project emerges large enough to meet a $500K minimum. That is a year-three decision.
