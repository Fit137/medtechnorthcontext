# MedTech North — Launch Social Campaign
## Final content + design concepts for Claude Design

**12 assets across three series.** Series A builds awareness (purpose, mission, why). Series B speaks to each of the four segments. Series C launches the first dinner. Every asset is final copy, ready to render.

---

## Instructions to Claude Design (paste this section with the assets below)

Build each of the 12 assets as its **own separate frame, individually exportable as a PNG**. No shared canvas, no collage. Master format: **1080 × 1350 (4:5 portrait)** for every asset, optimized for LinkedIn and Instagram feed. Use the existing MedTech North design system for all typography and color, the drawn four-marker family (founders, clinicians & researchers, capital & corporate, policy), the Canada Data Map assets, and the craft standards already established: no emoji, no icon fonts, no stock icons, no blur effects, custom-drawn SVG geometry only, three-layer composition (background field, midground structure, foreground copy and data), 8px grid, one dominant element per frame.

Social-specific rules:
- **Copy is typeset exactly as written below.** Do not rewrite, shorten, or add words to the on-image copy.
- **One idea per frame.** Headline dominant (readable at thumbnail size in a feed), support line secondary, footer strip constant.
- **Constant footer strip on every asset:** wordmark left, "Toronto · Canada-wide" right. This is the series' signature.
- **Series identity:** Series A frames use the Canada map and stat layers as their visual base. Series B frames use the four marker components at hero scale. Series C frames use the table and seat geometry. A viewer seeing any two frames in a feed should recognize them as siblings.
- Mark every placeholder in [brackets] visually distinct so nothing ships unfilled.

# SERIES A — Mission & Awareness

### A1 — The Manifesto

**On-image copy**
- Headline: *The room where Canadian health innovation actually happens.*
- Support: Founders, clinicians, capital and policy. One table. Built dinner by dinner.
- Footer: wordmark · Toronto · Canada-wide

**Design concept:** The Canada Data Map as the full-frame background at low intensity, dot-grid landmass filling the canvas edge to edge. The four markers converge along drawn leader lines toward a single glowing point at Toronto. Headline set across the upper third at maximum scale; support line beneath at a third of its size. The most restrained frame of the campaign; it has to feel like a statement, not an ad.

**LinkedIn caption**
Canada has world-class health research, ambitious founders, active capital and engaged policy leaders. What it doesn't have is the room where they meet on purpose. We're building that room. MedTech North launches this year: a curated network connecting the four groups that move Canadian health technology forward, starting with 40 seats at one table in Toronto. Follow along. The first dinner is being assembled now.

---

### A2 — The Problem

**On-image copy**
- Headline: *18 months to get 30 minutes.*
- Support: That's what it can take a Canadian founder to reach the right clinical lead. The pieces of the ecosystem exist. The connective tissue doesn't.
- Footer strip.

**Design concept:** Split composition. Upper two-thirds: two marker components (a founder mark and a clinician mark) placed at opposite edges of the frame with a long, broken leader line between them, the gap in the line landing dead center under the headline. Lower third: support copy over a faint structured field of scattered marks. The distance IS the graphic.

**LinkedIn caption**
A founder spends a year and a half chasing one conversation. A hospital innovation office fields hundreds of cold pitches and trusts none. Investors see recycled deal flow. Policy hears about barriers years late. None of this is a talent problem. It's a proximity problem, and proximity can be designed. That's what MedTech North is for.

### A3 — The Ecosystem Is Real

**On-image copy**
- Headline: *The 8th largest medical device market in the world.*
- Data row: 1,500+ medtech companies · 6,000+ life sciences companies · $344B in health spending
- Support: The ecosystem isn't missing. The room is.
- Footer strip. Micro-footnote: Sources: Medtech Canada, Invest in Canada, CIHI

**Design concept:** The stat-forward frame. Canada map upper half with the headline stat anchored across it in a drawn chip; the three-figure data row beneath as aligned typeset blocks separated by hairlines, each numeral dominant over its label. The support line sits alone at the bottom third at unexpected scale, nearly as large as the headline; it's the turn of the argument and the frame should make you feel it.

**LinkedIn caption**
Canada is the 8th largest medical device market on earth. More than 1,500 medtech companies. Over 6,000 life sciences companies, with the 4th most active biotech firms in the OECD. $344 billion in annual health spending. Every ingredient of a world-class health innovation ecosystem is already here. What's missing is the table where its four sides sit together. We're setting it.

### A4 — The Four Groups

**On-image copy**
- Headline: *Four groups. One table.*
- Labels (one per marker): Founders & operators / Clinicians & researchers / Capital & corporate / Policy & government
- Support: Every seat curated. Every group in balance.
- Footer strip.

**Design concept:** The marker family at hero scale: the four drawn components arranged in a 2×2 constellation filling the center of the frame at their largest rendering anywhere in the campaign, each with its typeset label beneath. Thin drawn lines connect all four into a quadrilateral. Headline above, support below. This frame doubles as the campaign's legend; after seeing it, every other frame's markers are legible.

**LinkedIn caption**
Ecosystems don't fail from lack of talent. They fail from imbalance: rooms full of founders with no one to validate, panels full of experts with no one building, capital circling with no signal. MedTech North holds all four groups at one table, in balance, by design. Builders, validators, funders, and the people who clear the path.

### C3 — The Principles

**On-image copy**
- Headline: *Rules of the table.*
- Lines: No stage, no slides. Nobody buys a microphone. Guests are never prospects. What's said at the table stays at the table.
- Footer strip.

**Design concept:** The stillest frame of the campaign, mirroring the website's principles sections: no map, no markers, no scene. The four rules typeset as a vertical stack with generous spacing, each separated by a short drawn hairline. A single small table glyph (drawn, from the system) sits above the headline as the only graphic element. Composition carries this frame entirely.

**LinkedIn caption**
Trust is the only asset a network really has, so ours is governed by rules we don't bend. No pay-to-pitch. No lead harvesting. Clinicians and researchers attend as guests, never as prospects, and conversations stay at the table so people can speak plainly about procurement, regulation and money. Boring rules, kept absolutely. That's what makes the interesting conversations possible.
