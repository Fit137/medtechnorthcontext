# ADR-002 — Never charge clinicians, researchers, students or policy

**Status:** Accepted. **Permanent.** This does not change in any phase.

## Decision
Clinicians, researchers, academics, students and public sector leaders attend at no cost, forever. Access is by nomination or invitation, reviewed individually.

## Why
They are the reason founders pay and the reason corporates pay attention. Charging them, even a token amount, erodes the signal the entire model rests on. The best of them would not negotiate or complain; they would quietly stop coming, and the founders would follow, and the corporates last.

## Considered and rejected
- **Charging a discounted rate.** Same failure, slower.
- **Harder eligibility gate to control cost.** Throttles the nomination flow, which is the cheapest acquisition engine, to solve a cost problem that barely exists. Dormant guests cost ~$25/yr; the real cost is unfunded events, not free membership.
- **Lightweight-only guest tier with paid dinner access.** The dinner seat *is* the magnet. Removing it removes the reason the scarce side shows up.

## The actual hedge
Composition-gated attendance, not entitlement. A guest is invited when a funded, balanced room needs their profile. Plus a guest-to-payer ratio band near 1.5:1, where exceeding it pauses guest *recruitment*, never guest access.

## Framing rule
"Free means chosen, not discounted." Never present this as a discount or a promotion.
