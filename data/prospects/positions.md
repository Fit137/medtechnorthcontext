# Who to Call, by Product

> **INTERNAL.** Companion to `schema.md`. The three products have three different buyers, and calling the wrong one costs a warm path you cannot get back.

---

## The rule that decides everything

**Call the person who owns the outcome, not the person who owns the company.**

A $2,500 meetup and a $25,000 convening are approved by people four levels apart. Going too senior is not "safer": a VP forwards you down, and you arrive as an internal task rather than as a peer. Going too junior wastes a quarter.

---

## Monthly Meetup, $2,500. They buy a stage and opted-in leads.

Budget sits in demand generation, field marketing or events. At small companies it is the founder.

| Priority | Title patterns |
|---|---|
| **1** | Head of Growth · VP Growth · Growth Marketing Manager |
| **1** | Demand Generation Manager or Director |
| **2** | Field Marketing Manager · Event Marketing Manager |
| **2** | Community Manager · Head of Community |
| **3** | Partnerships Manager, when the pitch is co-marketing rather than a slot |
| **3** | Marketing Manager, Canada |
| **Under 50 staff** | Founder · CEO · CRO. There is nobody else |

**What they are measured on:** qualified leads, pipeline sourced, cost per lead.
**Open with:** cost per qualified conversation, not brand.

---

## Calendar Dinner, $4,500. They buy proximity, no leads.

Budget sits in brand, industry or field marketing. The absence of a list is the thing to say early, because a demand-gen buyer will be disappointed and a brand buyer will be relieved.

| Priority | Title patterns |
|---|---|
| **1** | Industry Marketing Manager or Lead, Healthcare |
| **1** | Field Marketing Manager, Canada |
| **2** | Brand Marketing Manager · Head of Marketing, Canada |
| **2** | Business Development Director, Healthcare |
| **3** | Country Manager, Canada |
| **Professional services** | Business Development Manager · Client Relations Director · Practice Group Leader |

**What they are measured on:** relationships, positioning, share of voice.
**Open with:** who is in the room, and that nobody is pitched.

---

## Convening, $25,000. They buy a composed room built to their brief.

Budget sits with a vertical owner or a country GTM leader. This is the only product where the buyer usually has a number attached to healthcare specifically.

| Priority | Title patterns |
|---|---|
| **1** | Healthcare Industry Lead · Head of Healthcare, Canada |
| **1** | Director or Lead, Life Sciences and Health Care |
| **2** | Head of Field Marketing, Canada |
| **2** | Country Manager · General Manager, Canada · VP Canada |
| **2** | Director, Strategic Partnerships |
| **3** | Head of Executive Programs or Customer Programs |
| **Professional services** | Partner, Life Sciences · Health Industries Leader · National Practice Leader |
| **Partnerships-led firms** | Head of Partnerships · Partnerships Manager |

**What they are measured on:** pipeline in their vertical, partner conversations, market entry.
**Open with:** the composition guarantee and the floor of 25.

---

## The highest-signal target of all

**Anyone appointed to a Canada-based healthcare role in the last twelve months.**

They have a number, no local community, a budget, and a reason to move fast. Search for the appointment, not the title. This outranks every other targeting rule in this document.

---

## Do not call

| Who | Why |
|---|---|
| Procurement | They process, they do not buy. They arrive later and they arrive on their own |
| HR or talent | Unless the pitch is genuinely employer brand, which it usually is not |
| Sales representatives | No budget, and they will try to sell you |
| Global headquarters marketing when a Canadian office exists | Adds a review layer with no matching budget |
| The CEO of anything above 500 people | The reply is a delegation, and you arrive as somebody's task |

## The buying committee, mapped

From the existing partner playbook. Same three personas, now mapped to these titles.

| Persona | Title patterns here | What they need from you |
|---|---|---|
| **Evaluator** | Field or industry marketing manager, partnerships manager | The composition report and a cheap way in |
| **Recommender** | Marketing or BD director, healthcare industry lead | The comparison logic and the never list, which protects them too |
| **Signer** | VP, country manager, practice partner | Precedent and a founder conversation |

**Enter at Evaluator.** They are reachable, they own the process, and they carry you up. Entering at Signer means being sent back down without a champion.
