# Nearby GTA roles outrank US remote openings

The five research notes turn up 95 open or possibly open postings with scope close to Trexo Robotics' Event Marketing Manager role. Only about ten of them show evidence of being posted, or still open, on or after 1 Sep 2026, and almost none of the US remote roles can hire someone who lives in Mississauga. The best practical targets are in the GTA. **Trexo itself comes first, but search did not find the posting or its Ashby URL, so it needs a direct check** ([Built In Toronto](https://builtintoronto.com/company/trexo-robotics/jobs)). After Trexo come League's community and events role in Toronto, **Epineuron's events manager in Mississauga (posted 21 Aug 2026)** ([MedTech Innovator](https://jobs.medtechinnovator.org/companies/epineuron/jobs/90732257-marketing-communications-and-events-manager)), Survalent's user-conference role in Mississauga and Geotab's four-role events team in Oakville. In the US, the newest remote roles at device and digital health companies are **Doximity (published 19 Oct 2026), GE HealthCare (deadline 1 Nov 2026), Stryker (28 Sep 2026) and Medtronic (5 Oct 2026)**. Pharma congress roles at Gilead, Jazz and BioMarin fit the candidate's pharmacy training best. **Only two postings outside Canadian-only hiring explicitly accept a Canadian home base:** Charles River's trade show role (home office in North America or the UK) ([Charles River](https://careers.criver.com/job/Wilmington-Senior-Event-Specialist-Corporate-Trade-Shows-%28REMOTE%29-MA-01887/1428162200/)) and Kinaxis' North America field marketing role (US or Canada). A second pass on contacts covered the 56 High and Medium fit rows and named 77 people across 37 postings: 6 Confirmed, 39 Likely and 32 Unverified, 30 of them with a LinkedIn URL. The shared web search cap (200 calls) ran out before rows 16, 18 to 20, 21, 33 to 37, 44 to 46 and 53 to 56 were searched, and Low fit rows were not searched. Where no person was found, the cell gives the title the posting points to. Trexo's Head of Marketing, Jenn Henry Horowitz, may have moved on (a later search found a Jenn Horowitz at another firm), so check her profile before outreach. ## Conclusion

The Trexo role is close to unique in Canada. Small Canadian medtechs fold event marketing into clinician education and collateral, as Epineuron and Profound do. Larger Canadian health tech companies split it into community programmes (League) or sales-aligned calendars (PointClickCare). The candidate's pharmacy training is the differentiator in the medtech roles, while the MedTech North founder story fits roles built on invitation-only formats: executive dinners and roundtables at League, Assort, Fulfillment IQ, ContactMonkey and Aledade. The US remote pool is bigger, pays more (roughly US$85K to US$194K) and is fresher. Without US work authorization, though, it is mostly a networking map into those companies' Canadian affiliates, not an application list.

The fastest way to improve these odds is a short people-search pass, not more job searching. About a dozen postings already state the manager's title. Running one LinkedIn search per title would fill most of the empty direct-manager cells with confirmed names, and that turns this table from a list of openings into a list of people to call.
