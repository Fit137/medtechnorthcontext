# Canada: event marketing roles in healthcare, medtech, health tech, life sciences and pharma, with contacts

Research date: 21 Oct 2026. Researcher scope: Canada only (on-site or hybrid; remote listed last).

Method and hard limits of this pass (read first):
- WebFetch failed on the first attempt (`getaddrinfo ENOTFOUND jobs.ashbyhq.com`), so every finding below comes from WebSearch result snippets and the search tool's summaries of those pages. No posting page was opened in full.
- The shared WebSearch budget for this turn (200 calls across all agents) ran out after about 35 searches from this researcher. People searches (site:linkedin.com/in queries for each company) had not yet run when the budget ran out, so most contact slots are "not found". This reflects how far the search got. It does not mean the people don't exist. A follow-up query list is in the Q2 Gaps section.
- No URL or name below was constructed. Every URL appeared in a search result. Facts drawn from the user's brief, or from background knowledge, are labelled as such.
- Relative dates such as "Posted 5 Days Ago" come from aggregator pages captured on unknown dates. Treat them as weak evidence.

## Q1. Which Canadian healthcare, medtech, health-tech and life-sciences companies have open event or field marketing roles, and what are the posting details?

### Takeaway
Eight on-site or hybrid Canadian postings in scope were found, plus one remote-Canada role. Most are in the GTA (Mississauga and Toronto). Only one has a confirmed date close to the 1 Sep 2026 cutoff (Epineuron, 21 Aug 2026). The rest are 2026 or date unconfirmed. The required Trexo Robotics Event Marketing Manager posting did not appear in any search result, and no Ashby URL for it surfaced. The strongest scope matches after Trexo are League (Toronto: a flagship healthcare-leader event plus a year-round community programme), Epineuron (Mississauga medtech: conferences plus physician/KOL engagement) and Profound Medical (Mississauga medtech: global congresses and physician education).
