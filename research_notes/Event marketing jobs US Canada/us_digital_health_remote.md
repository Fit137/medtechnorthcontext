# US digital health and health-tech: remote event and field marketing roles (research date 21 Oct 2026)

Scope: remote US roles at digital health / health IT / healthcare SaaS / telehealth / care navigation / healthcare AI companies, matched to the Trexo Robotics Event Marketing Manager reference role. Medical device, robotics, pharma, biotech and diagnostics employers are excluded (covered by other researchers).

Method note for the report writer: WebFetch failed on the first try (DNS error, `getaddrinfo ENOTFOUND jobs.ashbyhq.com`), so every finding below comes from WebSearch result pages, mostly job aggregators that mirror ATS postings. The shared WebSearch budget for this turn ran out partway through the work (the tool returned "this turn's web search budget is used up"). That happened before any people searches could run. So the posting list is in reasonable shape, but **no named contacts were verified**. Each contact slot gives the target title and "not found", plus a ready-to-run search queue in Key Question 2. No names or URLs were made up. Every URL below appeared in a search result.

## Key Question 1: Which US digital health / health-tech companies have open remote event or field marketing roles posted recently, and what are the details?

### Takeaway
I found 10 live-looking remote (or remote-eligible) US postings at digital health companies that fit the reference scope. Only two have dates on or after 1 Sep 2026: Doximity (published 19 Oct 2026) and Abridge (listing dated 19 Oct 2026, though its remote status conflicts across sources). Spring Health (posted 20 Aug 2026, valid to 9 Nov 2026) is the next most recent. Most of the rest were posted between February and July 2026 and still show as open on aggregators. None of the postings says Canada or "North America" eligibility. Every one either states or implies US residence, and several explicitly require US work authorization, which is a material flag for a Mississauga-based candidate.
