Research date: 21 Oct 2026. Researcher scope excludes medical device/robotics and digital health/health IT employers.

Method notes for the report writer (read first):
- WebFetch failed (DNS: `getaddrinfo ENOTFOUND boards.greenhouse.io`), so every finding below comes from WebSearch result snippets and summaries, not from reading the full posting pages.
- The shared WebSearch budget for this turn (200 calls across all agents) ran out partway through. Because of that, **no person-level (LinkedIn) searches were run**. Every contact field below gives the target title and "not found". No names were invented and no LinkedIn URLs are given. The reporting lines that the posting text states are recorded where found.
- Posting dates: few snippets showed a posted date. Where a Jobgether offer URL was seen, its leading 8 hex characters look like a MongoDB ObjectId timestamp. Decoded, they give the date Jobgether first indexed the job (6a99f500 = 2026-09-03, 6a73ffd8 = 2026-08-06, 6a5fe500 = 2026-07-21, 6a506751 = 2026-07-10, 66eac237 = 2024-09-18). This is an inference, labelled "first indexed (inferred)" wherever it is used. Built In job IDs rise over time, and IDs near 10.5M line up with early Aug 2026 on the same decoding. That is also used only as a rough guide.
- Only one posting (ImmunityBio) has evidence of first appearing on or after 1 Sep 2026. All the others are 2026 postings, or carry no date, and are marked "date unconfirmed" or given their earlier date. Before applying, the candidate should check that each one is still open.

## Key Question 1: Which US life-sciences companies have open REMOTE event, congress, convention or field marketing roles posted recently, and what are the posting details?

### Takeaway
Ten remote US (or North America) life-sciences postings match the reference scope. Two are diagnostics/genomics (BillionToOne, Personalis), five are pharma/biotech congress or meetings roles (Gilead, Amgen, Jazz, BioMarin, ImmunityBio), two are CRO/services (Charles River, Precision for Medicine / Precision Medicine Group) and one is lab tools (Abcam, a Danaher company). Charles River's Senior Event Specialist, Corporate Trade Shows is the only one found whose text explicitly allows a home office anywhere in North America, which includes the candidate's Canadian location. Only ImmunityBio (first indexed about 3 Sep 2026) can be tied to the requested on-or-after-1-Sep window. Most of the rest date from Apr to Aug 2026 or carry no date.
