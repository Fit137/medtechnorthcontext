# Named contacts at 9 US healthcare companies with remote event marketing roles (rows 38 to 47, 76, 77)

Method note: WebFetch was blocked, so every finding comes from WebSearch result titles and summaries only. The shared web-search budget (200 calls per turn, shared across agents) ran out after 6 of 9 companies (rows 38 to 43, 47, 76). Rows 44 (BillionToOne), 45 and 77 (KARL STORZ) and 46 (Noah Medical) were **not researched**. Their JSONL lines give target titles as "not found". No names or URLs were constructed. Every LinkedIn URL below appeared as a search result. Machine-readable output: `contacts_part4.jsonl`, one line per row (12 lines).

Confidence key: Confirmed = named in posting, or title matches the reporting line with 2025/2026 evidence. Likely = title fits, currency plausible. Unverified = weak, dated, or aggregator-only evidence.
