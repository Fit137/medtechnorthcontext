# US medical device and medical robotics: remote event marketing roles and contacts (research notes, 21 Oct 2026)

Scope: remote (US) event, trade show, convention and field marketing roles at medical device and medical robotics makers. Digital health/health IT and pharma/biotech/diagnostics excluded (covered by other researchers). Reference role: Trexo Robotics, Event Marketing Manager.

Method note: WebFetch failed on the first attempt (DNS error, `getaddrinfo ENOTFOUND www.linkedin.com`), so every finding below comes from WebSearch result snippets and summaries only. No posting page was opened in full. After about 37 searches, the shared web-search budget for this session (200 calls across all agents) ran out. Planned searches for contact names, reporting lines, and official ATS links for some roles were therefore not run. The contacts fields reflect that: where no name appeared in a search result, the field gives the target title and "not found". No names or URLs were constructed.

## Q1. Which US medical device / medical robotics companies have open remote event, trade show or field marketing roles posted recently, and what are the details of each?

### Takeaway
Eight relevant remote US roles are current or probably current, plus two weaker leads that are flagged. Best scope matches to the Trexo reference: GE HealthCare (Event Manager, Advanced Imaging Solutions; application deadline 1 Nov 2026), Samsung HME America (Tradeshow and Event Manager), and Stryker (Manager, Trade Shows (Remote), posted 28 Sep 2026). None of the roles found is open to candidates based in Canada. All are US-remote, and GE HealthCare states outright that it requires US work authorization.
