# Named contacts for 12 Canadian event and field marketing roles (rows 23 to 37, plus 60)

Research date: 21 Oct 2026. Method: WebSearch only (WebFetch blocked). Machine-readable output: `contacts_part2.jsonl` in this folder (14 lines, one per row number; rows 31 and 60 share Kinaxis contacts).

Coverage note: rows 23 to 31/60 (Jane App, UHN Foundation, UHN, PointClickCare, MedMe Health, Chili Piper, Cardata, Kinaxis) were researched. The shared web-search budget for this turn (200 calls across all agents) ran out before row 33. FacilityOS (33), ContactMonkey (34), Lightspeed Commerce (35), SOTI (36) and Forum Ventures (37) were **not searched**. Their JSONL lines carry "not found (target: ...)" with the posting URL. Treat those as open, not as confirmed absences.

Tool behaviour note: the search tool declined direct "person name + LinkedIn" lookups as person identification, and `site:linkedin.com/in` queries mostly returned job postings instead of profiles. Only one LinkedIn URL surfaced in results (Purya Sarmadi, MedMe). Every other LinkedIn field is "not found". Names below come from press releases, company pages, job postings and speaker or profile pages.

## Who currently holds the target title at each company, and who is the most senior marketing leader?

### Takeaway
Most senior marketing leaders were identified for 7 of the 8 researched companies, with 2026 evidence for Kinaxis (new CMO Kristin Russel, June 2026) and Cardata (VP Growth Winnie Chen, January 2026). Direct-manager names were found for Jane App, MedMe, Chili Piper and Cardata. The stated or target reporting titles at UHN Foundation (Director, Creative), UHN, PointClickCare (Sr. Manager, Event Marketing) and Kinaxis (NA field marketing lead) could not be named.
